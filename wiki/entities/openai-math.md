# openai/math

**OpenAI Mathematics Manuscript Collection and Formal Proof Artifacts** is an open-source repository containing 722 mathematical research manuscripts across 372 result families, 162 Lean 4 formalized proofs, 811 Comparator challenges, and 10 detailed reasoning summaries generated by an internal OpenAI reasoning model.

- **Upstream live URL:** `https://github.com/openai/math`
- **Pinned HEAD SHA:** `adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a` (2026-10-06)
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0
- **Integration class:** **Submodule** (lives in `submodules/math` in the Kubona repository)
- **Role in Kubona:** Benchmark catalog, formal proof verification corpus, and analytical ground-truth anchor library for quantum many-body physics and combinatorial optimization.

## Generation Methodology

According to the repository documentation:
1. Posed ~4,000 open research problems to an unreleased internal OpenAI reasoning model.
2. Each result consumed an average of **3 hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute**.
3. Outputs were grouped into 372 result families where papers include principal results, companion proofs, consequences, or refutations.
4. Formalizations in Lean 4 were constructed to machine-verify claims using `comparator`.

## Platform Integration in Kubona

Kubona integrates `openai/math` across three rings:

1. **Science Discovery Catalog (`kubona.science.math_catalog`):**
   - In-memory structured index of all 372 families and 17 disciplines.
   - Enables agents in the [[five-ring-research-loop]] to search and retrieve open mathematical problems, LaTeX sources, and BibTeX citations.

2. **Analytical Physics Anchors (`kubona.anchors.math_physics`):**
   - Exact mathematical proofs for quantum systems (Heisenberg ferromagnet, SK model ground states, Haldane gap, Laughlin Hall state, MUB-6).
   - Enforces [[quantum-advantage-realism]]: quantum algorithms (VQE, QAOA) benchmark against exact analytical values before cloud QPU submission.

3. **Formal Verification Engine (`kubona.eval.comparator_eval`):**
   - Evaluates proof challenges against Lean 4 kernel standards.
   - Enforces strict axiom containment (`propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice`), rejecting cheat axioms and logging results to the [[provenance-and-reproducibility|ledger]].
